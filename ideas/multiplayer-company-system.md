# Multiplayer Company System

> A system for creating companies in multiplayer.

Tags: `🟩 lp`

## Details

A Multiplayer Company System. Create diffrent companies in multiplayer, invite players to your company.
Companies have there own balance, seperate from there owner/members balance.
Companies can have vehicles assigned to them (As company cars), and Company members can use them.
(Companies can have Company Cars, Boats, Aircraft, etc)
Members can also accept Tasks/Contracts on the companys behalf (If company/member permissions allow) and then company members can see tasks.
Any tasks taken on the company behalf that are completed, the monetary reward is added to the Company Balance.

Company owners/managers can issue individual paychecks to company members in return for work, Or set up profit share (So when contracts are completed, company members get a share of profit)
Diffrent profit share values can be assigned to diffrent company members.

Company Owners or Managers can also manage company member roles (Edit permisions, Fire, or Freeze Permissions)
